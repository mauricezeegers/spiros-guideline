A working checklist for the SPIROS 2026 reporting guideline for observational study protocols. Tick off items as you address them in your protocol, expand any item for its explanation and example text from the Explanation & Elaboration paper, and print or export a completed copy for your files. Progress is saved in this browser only. Nothing you fill in is sent anywhere or visible to anyone else, including us.

Based on the SPIROS 2026 Statement and Explanation & Elaboration papers (Mahajan, Zeegers et al.). This tool is unofficial and for working use during protocol drafting; always consult the published guideline.
